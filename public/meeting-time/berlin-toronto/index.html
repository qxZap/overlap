Questions about Berlin and Toronto time

What is the time difference between Berlin and Toronto?

Berlin is 6 hours ahead of Toronto for most of 2026. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Berlin is 5 hours ahead of Toronto.

What is the best time for a meeting between Berlin and Toronto?

For most of 2026, the best time is 15:00 to 17:00 in Berlin, which is 09:00 to 11:00 in Toronto, with 2 hours of overlap. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, the best time is 14:00 to 17:00 in Berlin, which is 09:00 to 12:00 in Toronto, with 3 hours of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Berlin and Toronto change clocks on the same day?

No. In 2026 Berlin changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October, and Toronto on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Berlin is 5 hours ahead of Toronto.

When it is 09:00 in Berlin, what time is it in Toronto?
