Questions about London and Seoul time

What is the time difference between London and Seoul?

Seoul is 8 hours ahead of London for most of 2026. From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, Seoul is 9 hours ahead of London.

What is the best time for a meeting between London and Seoul?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 07:00 to 08:00 in London (early for London), which is 15:00 to 16:00 in Seoul (great for Seoul). From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 07:00 to 08:00 in London (early for London), which is 16:00 to 17:00 in Seoul (fine for Seoul). These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do London and Seoul change clocks on the same day?

No. Seoul stays on UTC+09:00 all year, while London changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October 2026, so the time difference changes when London does.

When it is 09:00 in London, what time is it in Seoul?

It is 17:00 in Seoul for most of 2026 and 18:00 from 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December.
