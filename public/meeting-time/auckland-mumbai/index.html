Questions about Auckland and Mumbai time

What is the time difference between Auckland and Mumbai?

Auckland is 7.5 hours ahead of Mumbai for most of 2026. From 5 April to 26 September 2026, Auckland is 6.5 hours ahead of Mumbai.

What is the best time for a meeting between Auckland and Mumbai?

For most of 2026, the best time is 16:30 to 17:00 in Auckland, which is 09:00 to 09:30 in Mumbai, with 30 minutes of overlap. From 5 April to 26 September 2026, the best time is 15:30 to 17:00 in Auckland, which is 09:00 to 10:30 in Mumbai, with 1.5 hours of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Auckland and Mumbai change clocks on the same day?

No. Mumbai stays on UTC+05:30 all year, while Auckland changes clocks on Sunday 5 April and Sunday 27 September 2026, so the time difference changes when Auckland does.

When it is 09:00 in Auckland, what time is it in Mumbai?

It is 01:30 in Mumbai for most of 2026 and 02:30 from 5 April to 26 September.
