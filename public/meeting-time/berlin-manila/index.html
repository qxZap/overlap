Questions about Berlin and Manila time

What is the time difference between Berlin and Manila?

Manila is 6 hours ahead of Berlin for most of 2026. From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, Manila is 7 hours ahead of Berlin.

What is the best time for a meeting between Berlin and Manila?

For most of 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 11:00 in Berlin, which is 15:00 to 17:00 in Manila, with 2 hours of overlap. From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 10:00 in Berlin, which is 16:00 to 17:00 in Manila, with 1 hour of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Berlin and Manila change clocks on the same day?

No. Manila stays on UTC+08:00 all year, while Berlin changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October 2026, so the time difference changes when Berlin does.

When it is 09:00 in Berlin, what time is it in Manila?

It is 15:00 in Manila for most of 2026 and 16:00 from 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December.
