Questions about Dubai and Toronto time

What is the time difference between Dubai and Toronto?

Dubai is 8 hours ahead of Toronto for most of 2026. From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, Dubai is 9 hours ahead of Toronto.

What is the best time for a meeting between Dubai and Toronto?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 15:00 to 16:00 in Dubai (great for Dubai), which is 07:00 to 08:00 in Toronto (early for Toronto). From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 16:00 to 17:00 in Dubai (fine for Dubai), which is 07:00 to 08:00 in Toronto (early for Toronto). These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Dubai and Toronto change clocks on the same day?

No. Dubai stays on UTC+04:00 all year, while Toronto changes clocks on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November 2026, so the time difference changes when Toronto does.

When it is 09:00 in Dubai, what time is it in Toronto?

It is 01:00 in Toronto for most of 2026 and 00:00 from 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December.
