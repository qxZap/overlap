Questions about San Francisco and Seoul time

What is the time difference between San Francisco and Seoul?

Seoul is 16 hours ahead of San Francisco for most of 2026. From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, Seoul is 17 hours ahead of San Francisco.

What is the best time for a meeting between San Francisco and Seoul?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 15:00 to 16:00 in San Francisco (great for San Francisco), which is 07:00 to 08:00 the next day in Seoul (early for Seoul). From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, the best time is 16:00 to 17:00 in San Francisco, which is 09:00 to 10:00 the next day in Seoul, with 1 hour of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do San Francisco and Seoul change clocks on the same day?

No. Seoul stays on UTC+09:00 all year, while San Francisco changes clocks on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November 2026, so the time difference changes when San Francisco does.

When it is 09:00 in San Francisco, what time is it in Seoul?

It is 01:00 the next day in Seoul for most of 2026 and 02:00 the next day from 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December.
