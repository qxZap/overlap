Questions about Dubai and San Francisco time

What is the time difference between Dubai and San Francisco?

Dubai is 11 hours ahead of San Francisco for most of 2026. From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, Dubai is 12 hours ahead of San Francisco.

What is the best time for a meeting between Dubai and San Francisco?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 09:00 to 10:00 in Dubai (fine for Dubai), which is 22:00 to 23:00 the previous day in San Francisco (late for San Francisco). From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 09:00 to 10:00 in Dubai (fine for Dubai), which is 21:00 to 22:00 the previous day in San Francisco (late for San Francisco). These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Dubai and San Francisco change clocks on the same day?

No. Dubai stays on UTC+04:00 all year, while San Francisco changes clocks on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November 2026, so the time difference changes when San Francisco does.

When it is 09:00 in Dubai, what time is it in San Francisco?

It is 22:00 the previous day in San Francisco for most of 2026 and 21:00 the previous day from 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December.
