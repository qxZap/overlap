Questions about San Francisco and Tel Aviv time

What is the time difference between San Francisco and Tel Aviv?

Tel Aviv is 10 hours ahead of San Francisco for most of 2026. From 8 to 26 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Tel Aviv is 9 hours ahead of San Francisco.

What is the best time for a meeting between San Francisco and Tel Aviv?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 09:00 to 10:00 in San Francisco (fine for San Francisco), which is 19:00 to 20:00 in Tel Aviv (late for Tel Aviv). From 8 to 26 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 07:00 to 08:00 in San Francisco (early for San Francisco), which is 16:00 to 17:00 in Tel Aviv (fine for Tel Aviv). These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do San Francisco and Tel Aviv change clocks on the same day?

No. In 2026 San Francisco changes clocks on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November, and Tel Aviv on Friday 27 March and Sunday 25 October. From 8 to 26 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Tel Aviv is 9 hours ahead of San Francisco.

When it is 09:00 in San Francisco, what time is it in Tel Aviv?

It is 19:00 in Tel Aviv for most of 2026 and 18:00 from 8 to 26 March and from 25 to 31 October.
