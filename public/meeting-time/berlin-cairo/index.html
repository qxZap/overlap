Questions about Berlin and Cairo time

What is the time difference between Berlin and Cairo?

Cairo is 1 hour ahead of Berlin for most of 2026. From 29 March to 23 April 2026, Berlin and Cairo are on the same time. From 25 to 29 October 2026, Cairo is 2 hours ahead of Berlin.

What is the best time for a meeting between Berlin and Cairo?

For most of 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 16:00 in Berlin, which is 10:00 to 17:00 in Cairo, with 7 hours of overlap. From 29 March to 23 April 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 17:00 in Berlin, which is 09:00 to 17:00 in Cairo, with 8 hours of overlap. From 25 to 29 October 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 15:00 in Berlin, which is 11:00 to 17:00 in Cairo, with 6 hours of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Berlin and Cairo change clocks on the same day?

No. In 2026 Berlin changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October, and Cairo on Friday 24 April and Friday 30 October. From 29 March to 23 April 2026, Berlin and Cairo are on the same time. From 25 to 29 October 2026, Cairo is 2 hours ahead of Berlin.

When it is 09:00 in Berlin, what time is it in Cairo?

It is 10:00 in Cairo for most of 2026, 09:00 from 29 March to 23 April and 11:00 from 25 to 29 October.
