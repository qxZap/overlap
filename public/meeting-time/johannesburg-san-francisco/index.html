Questions about Johannesburg and San Francisco time

What is the time difference between Johannesburg and San Francisco?

Johannesburg is 9 hours ahead of San Francisco for most of 2026. From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, Johannesburg is 10 hours ahead of San Francisco.

What is the best time for a meeting between Johannesburg and San Francisco?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 16:00 to 17:00 in Johannesburg (fine for Johannesburg), which is 07:00 to 08:00 in San Francisco (early for San Francisco). From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 19:00 to 20:00 in Johannesburg (late for Johannesburg), which is 09:00 to 10:00 in San Francisco (fine for San Francisco). These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Johannesburg and San Francisco change clocks on the same day?

No. Johannesburg stays on UTC+02:00 all year, while San Francisco changes clocks on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November 2026, so the time difference changes when San Francisco does.

When it is 09:00 in Johannesburg, what time is it in San Francisco?

It is 00:00 in San Francisco for most of 2026 and 23:00 the previous day from 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December.
