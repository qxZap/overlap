Questions about Chicago and Hong Kong time

What is the time difference between Chicago and Hong Kong?

Hong Kong is 13 hours ahead of Chicago for most of 2026. From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, Hong Kong is 14 hours ahead of Chicago.

What is the best time for a meeting between Chicago and Hong Kong?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 09:00 to 10:00 in Chicago (fine for Chicago), which is 22:00 to 23:00 in Hong Kong (late for Hong Kong). From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 19:00 to 20:00 in Chicago (late for Chicago), which is 09:00 to 10:00 the next day in Hong Kong (fine for Hong Kong). These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Chicago and Hong Kong change clocks on the same day?

No. Hong Kong stays on UTC+08:00 all year, while Chicago changes clocks on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November 2026, so the time difference changes when Chicago does.

When it is 09:00 in Chicago, what time is it in Hong Kong?

It is 22:00 in Hong Kong for most of 2026 and 23:00 from 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December.
