Questions about Bangalore and Berlin time

What is the time difference between Bangalore and Berlin?

Bangalore is 3.5 hours ahead of Berlin for most of 2026. From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, Bangalore is 4.5 hours ahead of Berlin.

What is the best time for a meeting between Bangalore and Berlin?

For most of 2026, the best time is 12:30 to 17:00 in Bangalore, which is 09:00 to 13:30 in Berlin, with 4.5 hours of overlap. From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, the best time is 13:30 to 17:00 in Bangalore, which is 09:00 to 12:30 in Berlin, with 3.5 hours of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Bangalore and Berlin change clocks on the same day?

No. Bangalore stays on UTC+05:30 all year, while Berlin changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October 2026, so the time difference changes when Berlin does.

When it is 09:00 in Bangalore, what time is it in Berlin?

It is 05:30 in Berlin for most of 2026 and 04:30 from 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December.
