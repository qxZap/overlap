Questions about Kyiv and New York time

What is the time difference between Kyiv and New York?

Kyiv is 7 hours ahead of New York for most of 2026. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Kyiv is 6 hours ahead of New York.

What is the best time for a meeting between Kyiv and New York?

For most of 2026, the best time is 16:00 to 17:00 in Kyiv, which is 09:00 to 10:00 in New York, with 1 hour of overlap. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, the best time is 15:00 to 17:00 in Kyiv, which is 09:00 to 11:00 in New York, with 2 hours of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Kyiv and New York change clocks on the same day?

No. In 2026 Kyiv changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October, and New York on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Kyiv is 6 hours ahead of New York.

When it is 09:00 in Kyiv, what time is it in New York?

It is 02:00 in New York for most of 2026 and 03:00 from 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October.
