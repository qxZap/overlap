Questions about Cairo and San Francisco time

What is the time difference between Cairo and San Francisco?

Cairo is 10 hours ahead of San Francisco for most of 2026. From 8 March to 23 April and from 30 to 31 October 2026, Cairo is 9 hours ahead of San Francisco.

What is the best time for a meeting between Cairo and San Francisco?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 19:00 to 20:00 in Cairo (late for Cairo), which is 09:00 to 10:00 in San Francisco (fine for San Francisco). From 8 March to 23 April and from 30 to 31 October 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 16:00 to 17:00 in Cairo (fine for Cairo), which is 07:00 to 08:00 in San Francisco (early for San Francisco). These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Cairo and San Francisco change clocks on the same day?

No. In 2026 Cairo changes clocks on Friday 24 April and Friday 30 October, and San Francisco on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November. From 8 March to 23 April and from 30 to 31 October 2026, Cairo is 9 hours ahead of San Francisco.

When it is 09:00 in Cairo, what time is it in San Francisco?

It is 23:00 the previous day in San Francisco for most of 2026 and 00:00 from 8 March to 23 April and from 30 to 31 October.
