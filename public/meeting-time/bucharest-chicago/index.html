Questions about Bucharest and Chicago time

What is the time difference between Bucharest and Chicago?

Bucharest is 8 hours ahead of Chicago for most of 2026. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Bucharest is 7 hours ahead of Chicago.

What is the best time for a meeting between Bucharest and Chicago?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 15:00 to 16:00 in Bucharest (great for Bucharest), which is 07:00 to 08:00 in Chicago (early for Chicago). From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, the best time is 16:00 to 17:00 in Bucharest, which is 09:00 to 10:00 in Chicago, with 1 hour of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Bucharest and Chicago change clocks on the same day?

No. In 2026 Bucharest changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October, and Chicago on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Bucharest is 7 hours ahead of Chicago.

When it is 09:00 in Bucharest, what time is it in Chicago?

It is 01:00 in Chicago for most of 2026 and 02:00 from 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October.
