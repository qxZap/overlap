Questions about Sydney and Tokyo time

What is the time difference between Sydney and Tokyo?

Sydney is 2 hours ahead of Tokyo for most of 2026. From 5 April to 3 October 2026, Sydney is 1 hour ahead of Tokyo.

What is the best time for a meeting between Sydney and Tokyo?

For most of 2026, the best time is 11:00 to 17:00 in Sydney, which is 09:00 to 15:00 in Tokyo, with 6 hours of overlap. From 5 April to 3 October 2026, the best time is 10:00 to 17:00 in Sydney, which is 09:00 to 16:00 in Tokyo, with 7 hours of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Sydney and Tokyo change clocks on the same day?

No. Tokyo stays on UTC+09:00 all year, while Sydney changes clocks on Sunday 5 April and Sunday 4 October 2026, so the time difference changes when Sydney does.

When it is 09:00 in Sydney, what time is it in Tokyo?

It is 07:00 in Tokyo for most of 2026 and 08:00 from 5 April to 3 October.
