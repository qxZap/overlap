Questions about New York and Paris time

What is the time difference between New York and Paris?

Paris is 6 hours ahead of New York for most of 2026. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Paris is 5 hours ahead of New York.

What is the best time for a meeting between New York and Paris?

For most of 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 11:00 in New York, which is 15:00 to 17:00 in Paris, with 2 hours of overlap. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 12:00 in New York, which is 14:00 to 17:00 in Paris, with 3 hours of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do New York and Paris change clocks on the same day?

No. In 2026 New York changes clocks on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November, and Paris on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, Paris is 5 hours ahead of New York.

When it is 09:00 in New York, what time is it in Paris?

It is 15:00 in Paris for most of 2026 and 14:00 from 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October.
