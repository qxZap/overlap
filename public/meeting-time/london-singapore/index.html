  • London is on UTC+00:00 in standard time and UTC+01:00 in daylight saving time. Clocks go forward 1 hour at 01:00 on Sunday 29 March 2026 and back 1 hour at 02:00 on Sunday 25 October 2026.

Questions about London and Singapore time

What is the time difference between London and Singapore?

Singapore is 7 hours ahead of London for most of 2026. From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, Singapore is 8 hours ahead of London.

What is the best time for a meeting between London and Singapore?

For most of 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 10:00 in London, which is 16:00 to 17:00 in Singapore, with 1 hour of overlap. From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 07:00 to 08:00 in London (early for London), which is 15:00 to 16:00 in Singapore (great for Singapore). These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do London and Singapore change clocks on the same day?

No. Singapore stays on UTC+08:00 all year, while London changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October 2026, so the time difference changes when London does.

When it is 09:00 in London, what time is it in Singapore?

It is 16:00 in Singapore for most of 2026 and 17:00 from 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December.
