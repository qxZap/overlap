Questions about London and Los Angeles time

What is the time difference between London and Los Angeles?

London is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles for most of 2026. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, London is 7 hours ahead of Los Angeles.

What is the best time for a meeting between London and Los Angeles?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 15:00 to 16:00 in London (great for London), which is 07:00 to 08:00 in Los Angeles (early for Los Angeles). From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, the best time is 16:00 to 17:00 in London, which is 09:00 to 10:00 in Los Angeles, with 1 hour of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do London and Los Angeles change clocks on the same day?

No. In 2026 London changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October, and Los Angeles on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November. From 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October 2026, London is 7 hours ahead of Los Angeles.

When it is 09:00 in London, what time is it in Los Angeles?

It is 01:00 in Los Angeles for most of 2026 and 02:00 from 8 to 28 March and from 25 to 31 October.
