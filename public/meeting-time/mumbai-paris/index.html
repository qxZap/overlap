Questions about Mumbai and Paris time

What is the time difference between Mumbai and Paris?

Mumbai is 3.5 hours ahead of Paris for most of 2026. From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, Mumbai is 4.5 hours ahead of Paris.

What is the best time for a meeting between Mumbai and Paris?

For most of 2026, the best time is 12:30 to 17:00 in Mumbai, which is 09:00 to 13:30 in Paris, with 4.5 hours of overlap. From 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December 2026, the best time is 13:30 to 17:00 in Mumbai, which is 09:00 to 12:30 in Paris, with 3.5 hours of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Mumbai and Paris change clocks on the same day?

No. Mumbai stays on UTC+05:30 all year, while Paris changes clocks on Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October 2026, so the time difference changes when Paris does.

When it is 09:00 in Mumbai, what time is it in Paris?

It is 05:30 in Paris for most of 2026 and 04:30 from 1 January to 28 March and from 25 October to 31 December.
