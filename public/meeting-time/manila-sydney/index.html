Questions about Manila and Sydney time

What is the time difference between Manila and Sydney?

Sydney is 3 hours ahead of Manila for most of 2026. From 5 April to 3 October 2026, Sydney is 2 hours ahead of Manila.

What is the best time for a meeting between Manila and Sydney?

For most of 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 14:00 in Manila, which is 12:00 to 17:00 in Sydney, with 5 hours of overlap. From 5 April to 3 October 2026, the best time is 09:00 to 15:00 in Manila, which is 11:00 to 17:00 in Sydney, with 6 hours of overlap. These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Manila and Sydney change clocks on the same day?

No. Manila stays on UTC+08:00 all year, while Sydney changes clocks on Sunday 5 April and Sunday 4 October 2026, so the time difference changes when Sydney does.

When it is 09:00 in Manila, what time is it in Sydney?

It is 12:00 in Sydney for most of 2026 and 11:00 from 5 April to 3 October.
