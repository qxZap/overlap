Questions about Mumbai and New York time

What is the time difference between Mumbai and New York?

Mumbai is 9.5 hours ahead of New York for most of 2026. From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, Mumbai is 10.5 hours ahead of New York.

What is the best time for a meeting between Mumbai and New York?

For most of 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 18:30 to 19:30 in Mumbai (late for Mumbai), which is 09:00 to 10:00 in New York (fine for New York). From 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December 2026, working hours do not overlap, and the least painful 1 hour meeting is 19:30 to 20:30 in Mumbai (late for Mumbai), which is 09:00 to 10:00 in New York (fine for New York). These times assume 09:00 to 17:00 working hours in both cities.

Do Mumbai and New York change clocks on the same day?

No. Mumbai stays on UTC+05:30 all year, while New York changes clocks on Sunday 8 March and Sunday 1 November 2026, so the time difference changes when New York does.

When it is 09:00 in Mumbai, what time is it in New York?

It is 23:30 the previous day in New York for most of 2026 and 22:30 the previous day from 1 January to 7 March and from 1 November to 31 December.
